# Your first ceremony

A ceremony is a recurring ritual — the daily, the planning, the retro — run by a system agent that
calls the agents of your team. It works by voice or by text; the voice is optional and the app says
plainly what "off" means.

1. Open **Today** and press the ceremony card.
2. The agent reads first: the repository's instructions, the documents you pointed the workspace at,
   and the open activities. Then it opens the round.
3. Answer by talking or typing. Each answer can cite what the agent read; the sources are shown
   beside the reply.
4. When the round ends, the minutes are written as a document you can read, keep or send on.

The agents it calls and the shape of each ceremony are in
[Development cycles](/reference/cycles), and what the voice engines do is in
[Voice](/reference/voice).

**What you should see:** a ceremony running with the agent's sources beside each reply, and the
minutes at the end.
